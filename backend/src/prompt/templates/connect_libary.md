# LinkedIn RAG Response Templates

This file contains 50 genre-specific response templates. Each template follows the required variables and instructions.


## 01 Tech Leader

Variables:
- {{target_name}}
- {{company}}
- {{role}}
- {{industry}}
- {{mutual_interest}}
- {{reason}}
- {{profile_summary}}

Instructions:
- Start with a brief greeting (Hi/Hello + {{target_name}}).
- Mention a concise link to {{mutual_interest}} and the specific {{reason}} for connecting.
- Optionally reference {{company}}/{{role}} or {{industry}} if it makes message more relevant.
- End with a short call-to-action (e.g., “Would you be open to connect?”).
- Keep message natural and professional; total characters must not exceed 300.

Template:
Hi {{target_name}}, I enjoyed your insights on {{mutual_interest}}. As {{profile_summary}}, I’d love to connect to discuss {{reason}}—especially given your work at {{company}} as {{role}}. Would you be open to connect?
